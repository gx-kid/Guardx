Troubleshooting Guide

Kids Device
Not Showing?

If the Kids device isn't appearing on your Parent app after pairing, follow these simple steps to fix it and get back online.

For versions below v10.6 only
01

Go to Settings → Apps → Find "System Service" & Tap Uninstall

On the Kids device, open Settings, tap Apps, search for System Service, select it from the list, and tap the Uninstall button at the bottom.

02

Tap "Deactivate Device Admin"

A system prompt will appear. Tap Deactivate Device to confirm. The app will then be completely uninstalled from the phone.

03

Download the Latest Kids APK

Once uninstalled, open the browser on the Kids phone, visit guardex.site, and tap the Download button to get the updated Kids APK.

Go to guardex.site & Download
04

Set Up Using the Video Guide

Install the downloaded APK and follow the step-by-step setup video below to grant permissions and pair with your Parent account.

Important — Check Your Version First

Only use this method if your Kids app version is below v10.6. If your version is v10.6 or above, this guide does not apply. Please reach out to our support team directly for personalised assistance.
