Frequently asked questions

How long until I get my code?

Please check your email 2–3 hours after you submit. Our team reviews your submission and sends the promo code to the Gmail address you entered.

How do I redeem the promo code in the app?

Open GX Parental Controller, go to Settings → Redeem code, paste your code and tap Apply. Your free trial starts straight away.

Can I use both methods?

Yes! According to our rules you can use both methods. Leave a 5-star review for a 3-day trial and also post a promotion video to earn a longer trial.

What makes a video earn a longer trial?

We look at how clear and helpful the video is, then at views and likes. Honest, well-made videos about keeping kids safe online do best.

Why was my submission rejected?

Common reasons: the screenshot is not a 5-star review, the video is private or unrelated, or the submission looks fake or duplicated.
