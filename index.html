Set up in three steps

It takes about five minutes, and you only need both phones nearby once.

  1. Download the GX Parental Kids app

    Install it on your child's phone using either download button above.

  2. Set it up

    Open the kids app and follow the on-screen setup, including the permissions it asks for.

  3. Enter the 8-digit code in the parent app

    The kids app shows an 8-digit code. Type it into the GX Parental Control app on your phone to pair the devices.

Questions parents ask us

Do you sell my child's data?

No. We never sell your child's data or share it with anyone.

Is the data encrypted?

Yes. Everything is protected with end-to-end AES-256 encryption.

Is anything stored on your servers?

No. Information is live only. It passes through our servers between the two devices and is not saved anywhere on our side.

What happens if the app is modified?

Accounts using a modified or cracked version of the app are banned automatically.

The kids' device shows as offline. What should I do?

Open the troubleshooting guide above. It walks you through the steps that fix this in most cases.
