Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you configure and use the GX suite effectively.

Is GX Parent Control completely stealth and invisible?

Yes. After granting initial permissions, the app icon can be automatically hidden from the app drawer and home screen. It operates completely silently in the background without popups or battery drain alerts.

Which Android versions are supported?

GX Parent Control supports Android 5.0 Lollipop all the way up to the latest Android 14 and Android 15 releases with full compatibility for Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, and Google Pixel devices.

How secure is the monitored telemetry data?

All telemetry, coordinates, and media payloads are encrypted with 256-bit AES cryptographic protocols before transmission. Only your authenticated parent account holds the decryption keys.

Does the target phone need Root or special modding?

No root is required! All features, including WhatsApp logs, live location, microphone recording, and screen capture, work seamlessly on standard stock Android devices.
